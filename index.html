    About

    Creator with a systems mind

    I'm Teniola Sogbetun, a cinematic video creator and AI content specialist based in Lagos, Nigeria, known online as 10CTY.

    My work sits where face-to-camera authenticity meets AI video generation. I build content around genuine storytelling and relatability, breaking down Web3 protocols, AI tools and fintech platforms into stories that connect.

    I hold a BSc in Biochemistry from Lagos State University and bring range most creators don't: graphic design, sound production, HTML and CSS. I take complex ideas and make them simple, relatable and human.
